## 1. Scope
This policy covers the Markiewi Fitness mobile apps and web portal (the "Services") used by
our coaching clients, challenge participants ("Community Members"), and staff.

## 2. Information we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Name, email, phone, password/passkey, profile photo | You |
| Health & fitness ("consumer health data") | Goals, injuries/limitations, intake answers, body measurements, progress photos, workout logs, food and water logs, habits, check-ins | You, your coach |
| Device health data (optional) | Steps, workouts, sleep, heart rate, weight, active energy from Apple Health (which may include data from Apple Watch, Garmin or Oura) | Apple Health, only with your permission |
| Messages | Chats with your coach and support | You, staff |
| Bookings | Class and session bookings | Calendly, Eventbrite |
| Payments | Billing history, plan, payment status. **We never receive or store your full card number**; payments are processed by Stripe. | Stripe |
| Technical | Device type, app version, crash reports, usage events | Automatically |

## 3. How we use information
- To provide coaching: programming, nutrition guidance, progress tracking, communication.
- To run challenges (leaderboards show only your chosen display name and score).
- To process payments and manage memberships and class packages.
- To keep the Services secure, fix bugs, and improve features.
- To send service messages (reminders, billing notices). Marketing emails only with consent; unsubscribe anytime.

We **do not sell** your personal information, and we **do not use health data (including Apple
Health data) for advertising, marketing, or data mining.**

## 4. Who can see your information
- **Your coach** — your health and fitness data and coach messages.
- **Owner** — business oversight, as needed.
- **Administrative staff** — contact details, membership status, bookings and package balances
  only. Administrative staff **cannot** see your health data or coach messages.
- **Other participants** — only your display name, avatar, and challenge score/feed posts in
  challenges you join. You can hide yourself from leaderboards.
- **Service providers** under contract, solely to operate the Services: Supabase (hosting),
  Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (web hosting/security), Expo (push notifications), Resend
  (email), Sentry (crash reports), PostHog (product analytics), Calendly/Eventbrite (bookings).
- **Legal** — when required by law or to protect safety.

## 5. Apple Health
Apple Health data is read only with your permission, used only to provide the Services to you
and your coach, never shared with third parties for marketing, and never stored in iCloud by us.
You can revoke access anytime in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access.
